SCAM DATABASE

Know the Common Scam Types

Understanding common fraud techniques can help you recognize suspicious activity before becoming a victim.

Phishing

Fake emails, websites or messages designed to steal passwords, personal information or credentials.

Smishing

Fraudulent SMS or text messages containing malicious links or requests for information.

Vishing

Fraudulent telephone calls where criminals impersonate trusted organizations or people.

Mobile Money Fraud

Scams involving mobile-money transfers, transaction codes, fake customer care and payments.

Identity Theft

Unauthorized use of personal information to impersonate another person.

Online Shopping Scams

Fake sellers, fraudulent advertisements, counterfeit products and non-delivery scams.

Employment Scams

Fake job opportunities that request registration, training fees or personal information.

Investment Scams

Fraudulent investment opportunities promising unrealistic or guaranteed financial returns.

Protect Yourself From Fraud

01

Verify Unexpected Requests

Independently contact the organization using a trusted communication channel.

02

Protect Authentication Codes

Never share passwords, PINs or one-time authentication codes with another person.

03

Check Links Carefully

Inspect suspicious links and avoid clicking links received from unknown sources.

04

Report Quickly

Preserve relevant evidence such as messages, screenshots and transaction information.

This system is a frontend prototype based on the academic research project titled:

The proposed system combines user education, incident reporting, suspicious-message guidance, search and categorization, and administrative management of scam information.
